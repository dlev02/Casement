Casement 1.1 (6) — iPhone, iPad, Mac, and Apple Vision Pro

A WINDOW OF YOUR OWN
The new C-shaped casement icon supports the system's light, dark, clear, and tinted appearances on iPhone, iPad, and Mac. Vision Pro uses a layered circular icon.

CONNECT WITH CONFIDENCE
Computers prove their identity before sign-in. Casement asks before typing a saved Mac password over a home-network connection. Use Casement Host 1.4 or later.

CONTROL THAT FITS
New since build 5: PC Audio and display-fitting controls, clearer dictation levels, a show-password button, and better Settings and app-lock behavior when the iPad app runs on a Mac. Also recheck iPhone Duo laptop mode and compact iPhone controls.

WHAT TO TEST
• Install over your current TestFlight copy. Confirm saved computers, pairings, and settings remain available.
• Check the Home Screen or Dock icon in each supported appearance. On Vision Pro, check the circular icon and depth.
• Connect to both a Mac and a Windows PC with Casement Host 1.4 or later. Pair a new device and reconnect to a saved one. A changed computer identity should never be accepted silently.
• Try pointer movement, keyboard shortcuts, clipboard sharing, audio, and a file transfer in each direction.
• On iPhone Duo, fold, unfold, and rotate during a session. Check the desktop fit, keyboard, and session controls.
• On Mac, open Settings with Command-comma and switch tabs. For the iPad app on Mac, check app lock while moving between Spaces.

Report a problem from Settings/About and include diagnostics. Connections stay within your own Tailscale network. Host downloads: https://github.com/dlev02/Casement/releases
