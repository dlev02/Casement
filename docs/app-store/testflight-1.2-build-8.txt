Casement 1.2 (8) — iPhone, iPad, Mac, and Apple Vision Pro

A PC THAT FILLS YOUR MAC
In Full Screen the toolbar now hides until you point at the top of the screen, so a PC fills a MacBook's display edge to edge. With Fit Display on, the window resizes freely and the PC follows its shape. View > Actual Size still snaps the window to the PC's own resolution.

FIT DISPLAY, WHENEVER YOU WANT
Fit Display (Virtual Display for a Mac) is now a switch in the Session menu, the session menu on iPhone and iPad, and the device pane on the Mac. It takes effect right away, without reconnecting.

WHAT TO TEST
• On a Mac, connect to a PC with Fit Display on and enter Full Screen: the picture should fill the screen with no black bars and no toolbar until you hover at the top.
• Resize the window: the PC should change shape to match within a moment.
• Turn Fit Display off mid-session: the PC's own resolution and scale should come back. Turn it on again: it should fit again.
• On iPhone and iPad, flip the same switch from the session menu.
